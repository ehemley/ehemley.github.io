Hodge Atoms Learning Seminar

In a recent work, Katzarkov-Kontsevich-Pantev-Yue Yu introduce a new class of birational invariants called Hodge atoms to prove the irrationality of the very general cubic 4-fold. The theory of atoms lies at the intersection of several fields, combining ideas from Hodge theory, Gromov-Witten theory, Homological Mirror Symmetry, and non-Archimedean geometry. In this seminar, we will build up to the study of Katzarkov et al. 2025 and try to understand the basics of this new theory.
